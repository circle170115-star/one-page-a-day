Diary Calligraphy

A personal record · 2026

The days that pass with my child,
kept one page at a time

I write down the feelings that cross me while raising my child, and once a week I pick up a brush and leave a piece of calligraphy. It doesn't have to be good. This is not a place made to show anyone — it is a record for us to read again later.

01 — Parenting diary

Notes from the days with a small person

Out of ordinary days I keep only the scenes that stay: a morning that was too quiet, a question I couldn't answer, a hand I held longer than usual.

Wednesday, 11 November 2026 · 3 min

Early morning, before anyone else is awake

At six the living room is quiet the way water is quiet. My child's breathing slips out through the gap in the door, and I hold a cup that has not yet gone cold in both hands.

02 — Weekly calligraphy

One word a week, written slowly

After Monday's class I choose one word for the week and write it until my hand stops resisting.

천천히 — slowly

Monday, 9 November 2026 · week 46

Hansunji paper · sumi ink · brush no. 8 — three characters in one breath

The word this week was “천천히”, slowly. When it takes my child five minutes to get their shoes on, I stand beside them, look only at their feet and try not to hurry them. Writing it afterwards seems to hold that feeling in place. In the end the lines I drew without force are the ones that stayed.
